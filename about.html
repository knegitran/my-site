Kiyomi

Designer, storyteller, and visual systems thinker.

I’m Kiyomi, a multidisciplinary designer helping founders and small brands turn ideas into memorable visual experiences.

My work blends strategy, atmosphere, and clean design thinking to create systems that feel both elevated and useful. I care deeply about how a brand feels in the world—across website, packaging, messaging, and social presence.

I’ve spent the past several years collaborating with businesses that value original thinking, tactile design, and a distinct voice.
